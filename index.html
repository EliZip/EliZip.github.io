Hi, I'm Eli.

I am a person residing in Finland who is both an artist of many years and a developer of games. I often find myself blending these two passions into my work.

I've been drawing for as long as I can remember and have become very used to drawing characters and experimenting with different styles I enjoy. I'd spend hours studying the works of other artists and trying to understand their techniques until I've got a good grasp of them. Which in return helped me improve my own skills.

One day I found out people can make games without studios and was completely starstruck. I wanted to do that! And then quickly found out I would have to work on it for longer than a day. But instead of giving up I took up learning software engineering, and now I do what little me couldn't be bothered to do back then.

Game Dev Portfolio

Projects I have worked on!

Rise of the Fallen

Rise of the Fallen is a first person action game. Inspired by the idea of Doom but where you play on the side of the demons instead, you run through facilities and fight against the human forces trying to exterminate demonkind.

My role in the creation was main designer and artist and developer for UI, Logo designer and the environment builder for the different levels.

  • Unreal
  • 3D combat
  • UI and menu design, implimentation

Tenebris

Tenebris is a survival horror game. You play as a little girl haunted by her own mind and the monsters within it. Unable to go to sleep, you must navigate through your house. Accomplish tasks to help rest, but don't make too much noise.

My role in the creation was main designer and artist and 3D modeler and animator.

  • Unity
  • Horror
  • 3D modeling and animation

Way of Life

Way of Life is a puzzle platformer game. You play as both Fern and Oak after the tree of life disappears. They must work together to get through the realms of the forevers, the gods of their world, to unite them and create a new tree. Finding adventure and friends along with obstacles that challenge their abilities.

As things are I am the only person on this project.

  • Godot
  • Puzzle platformer
  • Sprite art and animation
